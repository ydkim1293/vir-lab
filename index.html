Visual Intelligence
& Reliability Lab

The Visual Intelligence & Reliability Lab (VIR Lab) at Hanbat National University advances visual AI for the real world through research in computer vision, deep learning, and reliable AI.

Research Interests

Our interests include, but are not limited to:

Visual Intelligence

  • Recognition, Detection & Segmentation
  • Multimodal Learning
  • Generative Models

Reliability

  • Robust Learning from Imperfect Data
  • Out-of-Distribution Generalization
  • Interpretability & Explainability
