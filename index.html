Research

What we work on

VIR Lab studies how to make visual AI both capable and trustworthy — from perception and generation to robustness and interpretability.

Visual Intelligence

Building models that perceive, understand, and generate the visual world.

  • Recognition, Detection & Segmentation
  • Multimodal Learning
  • Generative Models

Reliability

Making AI dependable when data is imperfect and the world shifts.

  • Robust Learning from Imperfect Data
  • Out-of-Distribution Generalization
  • Interpretability & Explainability
